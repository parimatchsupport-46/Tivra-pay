About TIVRA Pay

What the TIVRA Pay Help Center is and how it helps you.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

ℹ️ What Is TIVRA Pay Help Center?

TIVRA Pay Help Center is the official support website for TIVRA Pay users. It gives you a simple way to report payment and account problems and to follow up with the support team — all from one place.

🎯 Our Purpose

The goal of this Help Center is to make support simple and fast. Instead of searching for help in different places, you can raise a complaint, attach your payment proof and chat with support directly from this website.

🛠️ Available Services

  • 💰 Deposit Problem — report money not credited, pending or failed deposits.
  • 💸 Withdrawal Problem — report delayed, rejected or missing payouts.
  • 👤 Account Problem — report login issues, KYC verification or profile changes.
  • 💬 Online Chat — chat with the support team about any issue.

📝 How It Works — Step by Step

  1. Choose your issue — pick Deposit, Withdrawal or Account Problem from the homepage.
  2. Fill in your details — enter your name, UID, mobile number and the amount involved.
  3. Upload proof — attach a clear screenshot or payment image (up to 5 MB).
  4. Submit the complaint — your request is saved and a chat is opened for it.
  5. Follow up in Online Chat — track your complaint and send or receive messages anytime.

💬 About Online Chat

Online Chat lets you talk to the support team about your issue. To start, you only need your UID and registered mobile number. Inside the chat you can send text messages, images and files, and you can edit or delete your own messages. Some initial replies may be automated to acknowledge your message quickly.

📌 Details to Keep Ready

For a faster resolution, keep these details ready before raising a request:

  • Your TIVRA Pay UID
  • Your registered mobile number
  • Transaction ID (or UTR / reference number)
  • Amount, date and time of the transaction
  • A clear screenshot or payment proof

💾 Good to Know

This website currently stores your complaints and chats locally in your browser on your device — there is no online account or server storage yet. For full details, please read our Privacy Policy.

📬 Need Help?

Go to the homepage and open Online Chat to talk to the support team, or pick the issue type that matches your problem.
