Privacy Policy

How your information is collected, stored and protected on this website.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

🔒 Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information the TIVRA Pay Help Center collects when you raise a complaint or use Online Chat, and how that information is handled. It applies only to this Help Center website.

📋 Information We Collect

When you use the complaint forms or Online Chat, you may provide:

  • Name — to identify your request.
  • UID — your TIVRA Pay user ID.
  • Mobile number — your 10 digit registered number.
  • Transaction amount — for deposit or withdrawal issues.
  • Images and files — payment proof or screenshots you upload.
  • Chat messages — text, images and attachments you send in Online Chat.

💾 How Your Data Is Stored

This website currently works without a server or online database. All the information you enter — complaint details, chat messages and uploaded images — is stored locally in your own browser (using browser localStorage) on the device you used.

  • Your data is not uploaded to any server by this website.
  • Data stays on the same device and browser where you entered it.
  • Anyone with access to that browser profile may be able to see the stored chats, so please use your own device.

🍪 Cookies & Tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or any third-party tracking tools. No external service receives your data from this website.

🖼️ Images & Files

Uploaded images are compressed inside your browser before being saved, to use less storage. Each image can be up to 5 MB. Files and images are stored locally along with your chat, as described above.

🗑️ Data Retention & Deletion

  • Your data remains in your browser until it is removed.
  • You can delete individual messages or entire chats from the Online Chat page.
  • Clearing this website's data in your browser settings removes all stored complaints and chats permanently.
  • Deleted data cannot be recovered, because no copy exists on a server.

🤝 Data Sharing

Because your data stays in your browser and is not transmitted to a server, this website does not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties.

🛡️ Security Note

Local browser storage is not encrypted. Please do not share sensitive details such as passwords, OTPs, CVV or full card numbers in the complaint forms or chat. Only share what is needed to resolve your issue.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

If the website's functionality changes (for example, if online storage is added in the future), this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect it. The latest version will always appear on this page.

📬 Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, please contact us through the Online Chat option on the homepage.
